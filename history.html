源流 · History

由倉頡到速成 From Cangjie to Quick

中文有數以萬計的漢字，一個 QWERTY 鍵盤卻只有 26 個英文字母鍵。

人們究竟如何把兩者連接起來？而速成，又是怎樣成為香港人最熟悉的中文輸入方式之一？

Written Chinese uses tens of thousands of characters. A QWERTY keyboard has just 26 letter keys. This is the story of how people made the two fit, and how 速成 (Quick) became one of Hong Kong’s most familiar ways of typing Chinese.

  1. 傳說Legend

    倉頡造字 Cangjie invents writing

    相傳，倉頡是黃帝的史官，天生四目。

    傳說他觀察鳥獸的足跡和自然萬物的形態，從中得到啟發，創造出最早的文字。

    數千年後，一套中文輸入法亦以他的名字命名，那就是倉頡輸入法。

    Chinese legend credits 倉頡 (Cangjie), a historian of the Yellow Emperor who is said to have had four eyes, with inventing Chinese writing. According to the story, he was inspired by the tracks and forms of birds and animals when creating written characters.

    Thousands of years later, the 倉頡輸入法 would take his name.

  2. 1870年代1870s

    電碼 把漢字變成數字Characters as numbers

    早期電報系統無法直接傳送漢字，因此人們需要先把每一個中文字轉換成電碼。

    1870年代初出現的中文電碼，為漢字編配四位數字。電報員利用電碼簿，在漢字和數字之間互相查找；一些常用字的電碼，更會直接記在腦中。

    這是中文進入現代通訊科技世界的早期實用方法之一。

    Telegraph systems could not transmit Chinese characters directly, so each character had to be represented by a code.

    In the early 1870s, Chinese telegraph codes assigned characters four-digit numbers. Operators used codebooks to convert between characters and numbers, sometimes memorising frequently used codes.

    It was one of the first practical systems for transmitting Chinese through modern communication technology.

  3. 1910年代至1940年代1910s–1940s

    打字機 幾千個字，怎樣放進一部機器？The Chinese typewriter

    中文打字機面對一個很直接的問題：

    英文字母只有幾十個，但中文卻有數以千計的常用漢字。

    早期中文打字機因此設有大型字盤，放置數千個金屬活字。打字員需要移動機械裝置，在字盤中找出所需漢字，再把它印到紙上。

    1947年，作家兼發明家林語堂展示了他設計的明快打字機。

    使用者不需要逐一搜尋整個字盤，而是先輸入與字形相關的資訊，把可能的漢字縮窄到一小組，再從中選出所需的字。

    這種「輸入字碼 → 縮窄選擇 → 選出漢字」的思路，已經非常接近今天中文輸入法的基本邏輯。

    Chinese presented an unusual problem for the typewriter: instead of a few dozen letters, a machine needed access to thousands of characters.

    Early Chinese typewriters used large trays containing several thousand metal character pieces. The typist moved a mechanism across the tray, located the required character, and printed it onto the page.

    In 1947, writer and inventor 林語堂 (Lin Yutang) demonstrated the MingKwai (明快) typewriter. Instead of searching through an entire tray, the typist entered shape-based information and selected the desired character from a much smaller group of candidates.

    That search-and-select logic anticipated an important idea in modern Chinese input: enter a code, narrow the possibilities, then choose a character.

  4. 1960至1970年代1960s–1970s

    中文電腦化 電腦可以輸入中文嗎？Can a computer write Chinese?

    電腦開始普及後，中文再次遇上一個問題。

    當時主流電腦系統主要以英文字母及數字輸入為基礎。為了解決中文輸入問題，工程師曾經嘗試過很多不同方法，包括設計擁有數百、甚至數千個按鍵的中文鍵盤。

    但另一個方向逐漸變得更加重要：

    有沒有辦法，只用普通鍵盤，就輸入數以千計的中文字？

    答案就在於把一個漢字轉化成一小組可以用鍵盤輸入的字碼。

    As computers developed, Chinese posed another challenge.

    Most mainstream computer systems were designed around alphabetic and alphanumeric input. Engineers experimented with many alternatives for Chinese, including keyboards with hundreds, and sometimes thousands, of keys.

    But increasingly, the question became:

    Could thousands of Chinese characters be entered using an ordinary keyboard?

    The answer would depend on finding ways to represent a character using a much smaller set of keys.

  5. 1970年代1970s

    倉頡輸入法 朱邦復的方法Chu Bong-Foo's Cangjie method

    1970年代，朱邦復在台灣研發出倉頡輸入法，一般以 1976年作為其發明年份。

    倉頡不是根據讀音輸入，而是按照漢字的字形結構，利用一套特定的拆字和取碼規則，把漢字轉化成鍵盤上的字碼。

    整套系統使用 24個基本字根，分布在 QWERTY 鍵盤上。

    因為倉頡主要看的是一個字「怎樣寫成、怎樣組成」，而不是「怎樣讀」，所以無論你說廣東話、普通話，或其他漢語變體，都可以使用同一套輸入方法。

    In Taiwan, 朱邦復 (Chu Bong-Foo) developed the 倉頡輸入法 (Cangjie input method) in the 1970s, with 1976 commonly cited as its year of invention.

    Cangjie breaks Chinese characters down according to their visual structure using a defined set of decomposition and coding rules.

    It uses 24 basic components, or radicals, mapped across the QWERTY keyboard.

    Because Cangjie is based primarily on the shape of a character rather than its pronunciation, it does not require you to know how the character is pronounced in Mandarin, Cantonese or another Chinese variety.

  6. 1982年1982

    公開 讓任何人都可以使用Opened to everyone

    1982年，朱邦復放棄倉頡輸入法的專利權，讓其他人和電腦公司可以自由使用及開發這套輸入法。

    此後，倉頡逐漸在繁體中文電腦環境中普及，尤其是在香港和台灣。

    時至今日，主要電腦及手機作業系統仍然支援倉頡輸入。

    In 1982, Chu Bong-Foo gave up the patent rights to Cangjie so that the method could be freely implemented.

    Cangjie subsequently became widely available in Traditional Chinese computing environments, particularly in Taiwan and Hong Kong, and remains supported by major computer and mobile operating systems today.

  7. 1990年代以後1990s onward

    速成 把倉頡變簡單Quick: Cangjie made simpler

    完整的倉頡輸入，一個漢字最多可以使用五個字碼，因此需要花時間學習拆字和取碼規則。

    速成（亦稱簡易）把這件事大幅簡化。

    你不需要輸入完整倉頡碼，只需要輸入：

    第一碼 + 最後一碼

    然後再從候選字中選出想要的漢字。

    這種方法比較容易學，但代價是準確度較低，因為很多不同漢字都可能擁有相同的兩個字碼，所以候選字會更多。

    速成後來在香港尤其普及，亦曾廣泛出現在學校的電腦課程之中。

    Full Cangjie can use up to five codes for a character and takes time to master.

    速成 (Quick, also known as 簡易) simplifies the process dramatically.

    Instead of entering the full Cangjie code, you enter only the first and last codes and then choose the character from a candidate list.

    That makes Quick easier to learn, although less precise: many different characters can share the same two-key combination.

    Quick became particularly popular in Hong Kong and was widely taught in school computer classes.

  8. 今天Today

    手寫、拼音、語音 Handwriting, Pinyin, voice

    今天，在電話和電腦上輸入中文已經有很多選擇。

    你可以用手指寫字、按照讀音輸入、使用預測文字，甚至直接說話。

    倉頡和速成代表的是另一種思考中文的方法：

    它們不是先問一個字怎樣讀，
    而是先看一個字怎樣組成。

    Today, phones and computers offer many ways to enter Chinese.

    You can write characters with a finger, type according to pronunciation, use predictive input, or simply speak.

    Cangjie and Quick represent a different approach: instead of starting from how a character sounds, they ask you to look closely at how the character is built.

X 難：用來處理部分難以按照一般規則拆解的字形。

The other keys have special functions.

X 難 is used for certain shapes that are difficult to decompose using the normal rules.

Z is not one of the 24 basic radicals and has special or implementation-dependent uses in some Cangjie systems.

倉頡 → 速成 From Cangjie to Quick

速成的基本概念非常簡單：

保留倉頡碼的第一碼和最後一碼，把中間的字碼省略。

就是這個簡單的改變，讓倉頡變成一套更容易入門的輸入方法，代價則是每次需要從更多候選字中作出選擇。

Quick keeps the first and last Cangjie codes and drops the ones in between.

That simple idea turns Cangjie into a much easier input method, at the cost of having more characters to choose from.
